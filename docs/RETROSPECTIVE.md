# docs/RETROSPECTIVE.md

## What Inkwell Demonstrates, Lecture by Lecture

-Process & Requirements (L1-3): a real process model choice and a
  requirements baseline, not an assumed one.
-Design (L4-6): modular architecture, applied design principles and
  patterns, and a documented database schema.
-Construction (L7-11): implemented, code-reviewed, quality-assured
  increments, not just a finished snapshot.
-Testing (L12-14): a real, automated test suite spanning unit,
  integration, and end-to-end levels.
-Security (L15-16): threat modeling and a documented OWASP Top 10 audit.
-DevOps (L17-21): SCM discipline, containerization, and a full CI/CD
  pipeline actually running on every change.
-AI & Metrics (L22-23): a documented, reviewed AI-assisted feature
  (registration, logout, post authors), and a metrics program that makes
  claims checkable.
-Management (L24): a viable plan, schedule, risk register, and support
  strategy for the work that remains.
